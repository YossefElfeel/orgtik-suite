## Decision (2026-09-30)

Accepted as proposed: recharts 3 behind the library's wrapper; in RTL the time axis is mirrored (the period starts on the right, the value axis on the right).

## Context

- recharts is in six apps. Tasks is on 2.15; files, sales, hr, marketing and content are on 3.x.
- Marketing also ships chart.js 4 with react-chartjs-2.
- Charts need the data-visualization palette in both themes, RTL, and an accessible table view.

## Proposal

- **recharts 3**, used only through `@orgtik/ui/charts`. The wrapper provides:
  - the palette and axis, tooltip and legend styling
  - loading, empty and error states
  - a table view of the same data
- chart.js leaves Marketing, and Tasks upgrades to recharts 3.
- **The RTL rule:** time axes are mirrored in RTL, so the period starts on the right, and the value axis moves to the right side.
  - Numbers keep Latin digits (D-016).
  - The alternative, time always left to right as many financial charts do, stays open until native readers check both versions.
