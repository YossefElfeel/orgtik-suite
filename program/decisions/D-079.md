# D-079: CRM services, SaaS Console software, WM content

Status: accepted ownership split, 2026-10-09. Amends D-011, D-058 and D-059; implementation pending P3.

## Decision

CRM (`apps/sales`) owns OrgTik service catalog editing, service plans/prices/bundles/publishing, leads/deals/customers and service-request delivery. Build new DS v2 routes; do not revive the 17 retired SaaS routes. CRM's customer workspace remains distinct from OrgTik's internal catalog authority: a customer CRM admin cannot edit the public service catalog.

SaaS Console (`apps/admin`, working name) owns software lifecycle, plans/limits, regional pricing, commerce, workspaces/subscriptions/seats/usage, billing/support, connectors and system operations. Shared identity, staff, zones, currencies, taxes and gateways stay platform foundations; CRM reads them. `OrgTik Console` is the report's suggested display name, not an accepted rename.

WM owns website content/media/SEO. Product/price mirrors are read-only and identify `managedIn: crm | console` with authorized deep links. Tool administration belongs to the customer tool (D-080).

## Data and permissions

Keep `S.db.services`, service-kind `websiteOffers` and `websiteOfferBundles` in the existing platform store. Change editing ownership/permissions, not historical money or storage. CRM service writes require current OrgTik internal catalog permission; Console software writes cannot mutate service-kind rows. Existing common pricing calculation remains authoritative: two editing owners do not imply two price resolvers.

Move managed-service requests to CRM with a Console redirect; website leads and paid service orders create linked CRM records idempotently. The public catalog combines both business lines and overlays data in the existing website adapter with fallback/parity. J19 covers CRM publish → website reload, J20 software Beta → Live. D-059's managed-service meaning remains intact.
