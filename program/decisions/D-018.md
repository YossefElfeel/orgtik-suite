## Context

SOS has its own chat (`/chat`, `/chat/:id`). Chats (Somion HQ) is the suite's module for chat and meetings, with the SoSBar launcher and "Meet now" added in this program. Two chat surfaces confuse people and split conversation history.

## Proposal

- SOS's chat is **frozen now**: no refactor.
- In the SOS wave, `/chat` redirects to Chats and the SOS chat routes retire.
- If the backend has history, it migrates into Chats.


## Decision (2026-10-01)

Accepted by Yossef (ahead of the PM's answer to Q-008): SOS's 1:1 chat retires in favour of Chats.

The PM packet still asks the question; if the PM answers differently, this decision reopens.
