## Context

The specs define two surfaces that don't exist yet:
- **ADM-01…33:** OrgTik staff's cross-tenant back office. It needs 2FA, 8 staff roles and an audit trail.
- **USR-00…27:** the customer's dashboard (workspace, tools, billing, team).

What exists today:
- SOS already owns identity, launcher data and per-tool roles.
- Sales carries about 17 routes of SaaS administration (plan management, coupons, packages, subscriptions, invoices, tickets). Its zone and currency model conflicts with spec 03.

## Options

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| A. Build both in SOS | One app | Mixes staff-only, cross-tenant powers with customer screens in one bundle and one session |
| **B. ADM new, USR grows out of SOS, Sales stays a CRM** (proposal) | A security boundary between staff and customers; USR reuses SOS identity; no refactor of modules that will be replaced | One more app to run |
| C. Two new apps | Clean slate for both | Rebuilds SOS's identity and launcher work |
| D. Extend Sales | Its modules exist | Its pricing model contradicts spec 03, and it's a CRM for the customer, not OrgTik's back office |

## Proposal

**B.**
- ADM is a new app. Local-dev already reserves ports 3008 and 4008 for it.
- USR grows out of SOS.
- Sales keeps its CRM, and its SaaS modules retire when ADM and USR ship.

## Consequences

- Sales is the last wave, so its ~17 superseded routes are retired rather than refactored.
- SOS's wave becomes the USR foundation.


## Decision (2026-10-01)

Accepted by Yossef (ahead of the PM's answer to Q-008): ADM is a new app; USR grows out of SOS; Sales stays a CRM (its plan, coupon, invoice and ticket modules retire when ADM/USR ship).

The PM packet still asks the question; if the PM answers differently, this decision reopens.
