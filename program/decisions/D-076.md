# D-076: bring presentation commitments into ordinary screens

Status: accepted scope, 2026-10-09. Amends D-073's presentation delivery location, retaining its mock/provider limits and frozen foundations.

## Decision

Migrate all 72 existing commitments to ordinary tool/account screens. `/presentation` leaves production navigation as each replacement is verified; Demo Panel remains only for controlled prototype states. Search, notifications and cross-tool writes use the selected workspace and owner-domain mock handlers. A presentation fixture or overlay is never evidence that a feature works on an ordinary route.

Keep the six states (normal, loading, empty, error, no-access, read-only), five catalogs, RTL, light/dark, mobile and tablet verification. Keep external payment, mail, AI, signatures, analytics, calls and regulatory behavior visibly simulated. Do not erase historical presentation evidence or describe those 72 package-covered commitments as 72 individually accepted backend features.

## Delivery

Pilot Tasks, then Account, HR, CRM, Chats, Files, Marketing, Console and WM. A tool package includes its first-run flow, connected-feature states, in-tool administration, approved Tier B enhancements and representative ordinary-route evidence. Remove old UI kits only after equivalent shared-library components work.

The route/feature classification in the v3 plan is an audit baseline; [the P0 report](../v3-report.html) distinguishes it from new implementation. P4 acceptance requires a flow and captures per tool, not the existence of a new page.
