## Decision (2026-09-30)

**A per-person setting, defaulting to Latin digits (0–9)**, chosen by Yossef at the G0 review (the draft proposed Latin only).

What follows:
- The profile (USR-23, and SOS's profile until then) gets a "Digits" choice: Latin 0–9 (default) or Arabic-Indic ٠–٩. It's shown for Arabic; other languages always use Latin.
- `useFormatter()` reads the setting and formats numbers, money, percentages and dates with `ar-u-nu-latn` or `ar-u-nu-arab`.
- Numeric inputs accept both digit sets and store Latin, so a value typed either way is the same number.
- Identifiers that are text rather than numbers (invoice numbers, IDs, codes, email, URLs) keep the characters they were issued with.
- Visual tests cover both settings in Arabic.

## Context

- Depending on the locale, `Intl` formats Arabic with Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣).
- Mixed digit systems in tables, money, IDs and dates are hard to scan.
- Copying them into other systems breaks.

## Proposal

- Latin digits everywhere: format with `ar-u-nu-latn` in `useFormatter()`.
- Dates, money, percentages, IDs and phone numbers all use Latin digits.
- Numeric inputs (money, percentages, phone, email, URL) stay left-to-right inside RTL layouts.
