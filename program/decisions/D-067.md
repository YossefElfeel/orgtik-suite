# D-067: custom-plan quotes allocate their total to explicit per-tool prices

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | **Accepted** (2026-10-07, roadmap implementation) |
| Decided by | Yossef |
| Refines | D-061, R0 custom-plan acceptance |

## Decision

Staff specify a price for each tool in a custom-plan quote. Those prices add up to the quote's total. The platform never invents an equal or proportional split of an aggregate quote.

The quote carries explicit per-tool lines in the workspace currency, with the chosen billing term and agreed seat capacity. Validate that every requested tool appears once and that the currency-rounded line sum matches the quoted total. A legacy single-tool quote has one unambiguous line; a legacy multi-tool quote must be allocated by staff before acceptance.

## Consequences

- Acceptance materializes each tool's workspace-private plan from its quoted allocation, exposing it for ordinary checkout. Acceptance never creates an unpaid active subscription line. Successful mock checkout creates the active lines; repeated acceptance is idempotent and cannot duplicate private plans or paid lines.
- The quoted amount is the full term price for the agreed capacity, not a unit amount to multiply by the seat count again.
- Staff and customer quote views show the tool allocations and total before acceptance. Changed capacity needs a new explicit quote rather than an invented price.
- Quotes, private plans and subscriptions remain mock prototype behavior; live payments remain deferred under D-063.
