## Context

- The specs' Arabic mixes MSA with Egyptian dialect: «اتمسح», «اتحلت», «مقفولة», «موظف بيشوف الحساب».
- The pricing zones (eg, gcc, eu, row) show customers across Egypt and the Gulf.
- Billing, legal and email copy has to read as formal and unambiguous.

## Proposal

- **Plain Modern Standard Arabic** for all UI, email and PDF copy. No dialect.
- The tone is direct, speaks to the reader, and is gender-neutral where the language allows.
- Terms come from the glossary (`../glossary/`).
- The status labels in `../status-matrix/` already replace the spec's dialect labels.


## Decision (2026-10-01)

Accepted by Yossef (ahead of the PM's answer to Q-007): Modern Standard Arabic for all UI, email and PDF copy.

The PM packet still asks the question; if the PM answers differently, this decision reopens.
