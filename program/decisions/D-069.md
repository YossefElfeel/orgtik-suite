# D-069: explicit persistent billing lifecycle commands

Status: accepted implementation choice for the approved R1 roadmap,2026-10-07.

Renewals, failed-payment progression, retries, recovery and seat changes run as explicit commands through the shared atomic writer. Ordinary reads do not advance billing. A recorded cycle/attempt produces at most one invoice, payment and notice, including after a lost response or cold start. Current billing authority is checked before receipt reuse.

2026-10-08 refinement: [D-070](D-070.md)/[A15](../phase-f/amendment-A15.md) also settles already-due transitions in trusted request preflight. It observes the existing clock and commits independently of the caller before authorizing their request; it never advances that clock. The original explicit command and receipt policy remains for recovery, seat purchases and operator runs. The historical ordinary-read limitation above is superseded by this refinement.

Preserve the existing seven-day grace and cancellation on day37; retries occur on days1/3/5. Late runs keep the original due/grace/cancellation boundaries. The affected line stops writes at read-only; recovery uses the recorded obligation and currency. Ordinary renewal preserves stored purchased quantity. An increase changes capacity only after successful mock payment; a reduction waits until renewal and must cover assignments and reservations.

Use the shared subscription proration and regional tax calculations. Keep the website full-period savings policy and immutable paid orders separate. All current invoice allocators advance past historical invoice numbers and the lifecycle ledger. Live gateways and regulatory submission remain deferred; the command output is deterministic mock behavior.
