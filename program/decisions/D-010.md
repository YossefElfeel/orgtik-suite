## Decision (2026-09-30)

**IBM Plex Sans Arabic**, chosen by Yossef at the G0 review (the draft recommended Noto Kufi Arabic).

What follows:
- `--ot-font-display` and `--ot-font-body` put IBM Plex Sans Arabic first, restricted to Arabic characters with `unicode-range`, then Montserrat (display) or Arial (body).
- Weights 400, 500, 600 and 700.
- The library ships it through both font routes from the spike (`next/font` for Next apps, `@font-face` for other hosts).
- Its humanist forms read well at 12–14px. The specimen shows how it sits next to Montserrat's geometry; the type roles tune size and line height for Arabic, and native readers check them in the component tests.

## Context

The brand font, Montserrat, has no Arabic. Every dashboard needs Arabic from day one (D-001), in light and dark themes (D-003), in dense UI such as tables and 32px buttons.

## Options

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Noto Kufi Arabic** (proposal) | Geometric kufi forms that sit well next to Montserrat's geometry; wide weight range; open licence | Kufi forms can feel formal in long text; check body sizes |
| Alexandria | Geometric, drawn for Arabic and Latin together; variable weights | Needs checking at 12–13px in dense tables |
| IBM Plex Sans Arabic | Very readable at small sizes | Humanist forms that pair with Plex, less with Montserrat |

## Proposal

- **Noto Kufi Arabic**, subject to the specimen review.
- It loads with an Arabic-only `unicode-range`, so Latin pages don't download it.
- `size-adjust` matches its visual size to Montserrat's.
- Weights 400, 500, 600 and 700.
- Arabic type roles set tracking to 0 and never use uppercase.

## Consequences

- One `--ot-font-*` stack per role, with Arabic first.
- The website only gets Arabic later, when the owner signs off a mirrored layout (plan S5).

## Revisit if

- Native readers find body text tiring at 13–14px.
- The weights don't hold up in dark mode.
