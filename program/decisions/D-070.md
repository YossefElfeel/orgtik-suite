# D-070: trusted due transitions and durable staff authority

Status: accepted implementation refinement of the approved R1 roadmap,2026-10-08.

Observe the server's existing business clock before authorizing ordinary workspace requests. Settle due offboarding/custody first and billing boundaries second, in separate atomic realm checkpoints. A rejected caller or a later billing failure cannot restore departed access. Repeated reads with unchanged state create no new lifecycle receipts. Immutable assets and operational health/status probes remain non-destructive. Do not advance or reset the shared clock.

One system CAS conflict reloads canonical accounts, platform and app state before one retry; storage failure and a second conflict remain failures. User mutations retain their own normal conflict policy. OrgTik staff departures increment a durable authority epoch and retain a revocation marker even when historical role records remain. Ordinary HR rehire never restores internal staff authority. A late loss of the last qualified replacement records recovery while ending departed access; deliberate recovery/reappointment remains a separate unfinished surface.
