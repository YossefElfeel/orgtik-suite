# D-046: Chats keeps its accent presets (each person chooses Chats' accent colour); the rest of Chats follows the brand

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | **Accepted** (2026-10-01) |
| Decided by | Yossef |

## Decision

Chats keeps its accent presets (each person chooses Chats' accent colour); the rest of Chats follows the brand.

## Consequence

The Chats wave maps the presets onto tokens instead of removing them.
